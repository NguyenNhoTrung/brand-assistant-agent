# Skill: creative_brief

**When to use:** the user asks for a creative brief, ad concept, ad script or campaign creative for a product and/or channel.

**Structure** (fill every field of the schema):
- `objective`, `target_audience`, `key_insight` — grounded in customer research (cite; mention sample size/date when citing research).
- `single_minded_proposition` — ONE compliant sentence the whole ad ladders up to.
- `reasons_to_believe` — product facts (ingredients, price, texture, approved claims) from the product sheet.
- `tone_and_voice` — from the brand voice guideline.
- `mandatory_inclusions` — required disclaimers/statements for this product or format, copied verbatim (e.g. the SPF usage statement; "Results may vary." for testimonials).
- `do_not_say` — internal list of wording the team must avoid (forbidden claims from the product sheet / policy).
- `hooks_to_test` — 2–4 opening hooks (first 3 seconds) inspired by creative learnings; compliant.
- `channels_and_formats`, `success_metrics` — from creative learnings / SOPs when available.
- `open_questions` — anything the KB does not answer (budget, dates, offer…).
- `compliance_notes` — one note per non-compliant claim in the request, with 2–3 compliant alternatives.

**Quality bar:** specific to the product and channel, every claim traceable to a source, zero rule violations in publishable fields.
