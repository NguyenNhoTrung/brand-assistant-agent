# Q2 2026 Customer Survey — Key Findings

## Method

- Online survey, fielded 2026-05-10 to 2026-06-05.
- **n = 1,200** US customers who purchased in the last 12 months.
- Margin of error ±2.8% at 95% confidence.

## Top Pain Points

| Rank | Pain point | % of respondents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sticky texture** of serums and moisturizers | 41% |
| 2 | Price | 27% |
| 3 | Not seeing results fast enough | 19% |
| 4 | Packaging hard to recycle | 13% |

### Notes on texture

Most mentions refer to layering serum + moisturizer in the morning under makeup. Oily-skin respondents were twice as likely to name texture.

## Purchase Drivers

1. Ingredient transparency (58%)
2. Dermatologist recommendation (34%)
3. TikTok / creator content (29%)

## Recommendations

- Lead with lightweight, non-sticky texture in serum creative.
- Test "layers under makeup" demos in UGC.
