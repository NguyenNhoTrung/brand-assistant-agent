# Review Mining — H1 2026

## Method

- 3,450 reviews from the website and Amazon, January–June 2026.
- Average rating: **4.4 / 5**.
- Themes coded manually by the insights team; quotes below are verbatim and unverified customer content.

## Positive Themes

| Theme | Share of reviews |
|---|---|
| Brightening / glow | 34% |
| Gentle, no irritation | 26% |
| Clean ingredient list | 18% |

## Negative Themes

| Theme | Share of reviews |
|---|---|
| Sticky feel when layering | 22% |
| Price | 15% |
| Pump packaging breaks | 6% |
