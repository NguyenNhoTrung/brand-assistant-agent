# 2025 Customer Interviews — Synthesis

## Method

- 1:1 video interviews, July 2025.
- **n = 18** customers (12 repeat buyers, 6 lapsed).

## Top Pain Points

| Rank | Pain point | Interviewees mentioning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Price** — "premium for a brand I'm still testing" | 11 of 18 |
| 2 | Unclear routine order | 7 of 18 |
| 3 | Texture | 4 of 18 |

## Quotes

- "I love it, but $38 for a serum makes me hesitate on the reorder."
- "I never know if the serum goes before or after the cream."

## Implications

- Explore bundles and subscription pricing.
- Add routine-order guidance on packaging and PDPs.
