# SOP — Customer Complaints

## Response Times

| Channel | First response |
|---|---|
| Email / help desk | within 24 hours |
| Social DM | within 4 business hours |
| Public review reply | within 48 hours |

## Handling Steps

### 1. Acknowledge

Thank the customer, restate the issue, never argue.

### 2. Classify

- **Product reaction** (redness, irritation) → escalate to the safety team the same day; advise stopping use; never give medical advice.
- **Shipping / damaged item** → replacement or refund.
- **Texture / preference** → offer an exchange.

### 3. Resolve

- 30-day satisfaction guarantee: full refund, no return needed for orders under $50.
- Log the case in the help desk with tag and product ID.

### 4. Close the loop

Monthly complaint summary goes to product and marketing teams.

## Do Not

- Do not promise a product will "fix" a skin condition.
- Do not share other customers' data.
