# US Shipping Policy

## Coverage

We currently ship **only within the contiguous United States** (48 states). Alaska, Hawaii, US territories and international destinations are not covered by this policy.

## Delivery Times

| Method | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 2–5 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.95 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.95 |

Orders placed before 1 pm ET on business days ship the same day.

## Returns

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days. Prepaid return label included.
