# SOP — Campaign Launch

## Scope

Applies to every paid campaign (Meta, TikTok, Google, Amazon) with a budget above $5,000.

## Timeline

| T-minus | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| T-21 days | Campaign brief approved (objective, audience, KPI, budget) | Marketing lead |
| T-14 days | Creative brief + concepts | Creative team |
| T-10 days | Creative review (see SOP — Creative Review) | Brand + Compliance |
| T-5 days | Tracking: UTM, pixel, conversion events QA | Growth |
| T-3 days | Final assets uploaded, ad accounts set up | Media buyer |
| T-0 | Launch | Media buyer |
| T+3 days | First performance check | Growth |
| T+14 days | Post-launch review + learnings logged | Marketing lead |

## Steps

### 1. Brief

Use the creative brief template. Every brief must state objective, target audience, key message, mandatory inclusions and KPIs.

### 2. Compliance gate

No asset goes live without compliance sign-off. Block-level rule violations stop the launch.

### 3. Launch checklist

- Budget pacing set (no more than 30% of budget in the first 3 days).
- At least 3 creative variants per ad set.
- Discount codes checked against the 25% cap (CMP-006).

### 4. Post-launch

Log results in the creative learnings library within 14 days.
