# SOP — Creative Review

## Who Reviews

1. **Brand reviewer** — voice, visual identity.
2. **Compliance reviewer** — claims policy (CMP/BRD rules).
3. **Product marketing** — product facts, prices, ingredients.

## Review Steps

### 1. Self-check by creator

Run the claims checklist: no medical claims, clinical claims only with a study, disclaimers present, SPF statement for sunscreen.

### 2. Brand review (1 business day)

Check tone against Brand Voice Guidelines and visuals against Visual Identity Guidelines.

### 3. Compliance review (2 business days)

- Block violations → asset returned, must be revised.
- Warn violations → reviewer may approve with a written note.

### 4. Final approval

Approved assets are tagged `approved` in the DAM with the reviewer names and date.

## Turnaround

Standard: 3 business days end-to-end. Rush (24 h) only with marketing lead approval.
