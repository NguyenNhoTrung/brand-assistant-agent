# Gentle Gel Cleanser — Product Sheet

## Overview

A low-foam, pH-balanced (5.5) gel cleanser that removes sunscreen and daily grime without a tight feeling.

## Pricing and Size

| SKU | Size | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| gel-cleanser-150 | 150 ml | **$24** |

## Key Ingredients

- **Mild amino-acid surfactants** (sodium cocoyl glycinate).
- **Green tea extract**.
- **Glycerin + allantoin**.
- Fragrance-free, sulfate-free.

## Skin Types

- All skin types, including sensitive and oily skin.

## How to Use

Massage onto damp skin morning and night, rinse with lukewarm water.

## Approved Claims

- "Cleanses without stripping."
- "Leaves skin feeling soft, not tight."
- "Gentle enough for twice-daily use."

## Claims Not Allowed

- "Eliminates acne" or "clears breakouts" (CMP-001).
- "Clinically proven" — no studies on file (CMP-002).
