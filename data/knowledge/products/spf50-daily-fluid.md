# SPF 50 Daily Fluid — Product Sheet

## Overview

A sheer, no-white-cast broad-spectrum SPF 50 fluid for daily wear under makeup.

## Pricing and Size

| SKU | Size | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| spf50-daily-fluid | 50 ml | **$32** |

## Key Ingredients

- **Zinc oxide + avobenzone** — broad-spectrum UVA/UVB filters.
- **Niacinamide 2%**.
- **Vitamin E**.
- Fragrance-free.

## Skin Types

- All skin types; especially popular with oily and combination skin (matte finish).

## How to Use

Apply generously as the last step of your morning routine, 15 minutes before sun exposure.

## Mandatory Statement

Every ad, social post and creative brief for this product **must** include, verbatim:

> Reapply every 2 hours and after swimming.

This is required by compliance rule **CMP-005** (block).

## Approved Claims

- "Broad-spectrum SPF 50 protection."
- "No white cast."
- "Wears well under makeup."

## Claims Not Allowed

- "Sunblock", "waterproof" or "all-day protection" — not permitted for sunscreens.
- "Prevents skin cancer" (CMP-001).
