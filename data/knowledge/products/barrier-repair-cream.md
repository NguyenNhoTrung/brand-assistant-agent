# Barrier Repair Cream — Product Sheet

## Overview

A rich but non-greasy moisturizer with ceramides and colloidal oatmeal that supports the skin's moisture barrier.

## Pricing and Size

| SKU | Size | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| barrier-cream-50 | 50 ml | **$36** |

## Key Ingredients

- **Ceramide complex (NP, AP, EOP)** — supports the moisture barrier.
- **2% colloidal oatmeal** — soothes the feeling of dry, tight skin.
- **Squalane** — lightweight emollient.
- **Panthenol (pro-vitamin B5)**.
- Fragrance-free, non-comedogenic.

## Skin Types

- **Best for:** dry, sensitive and dehydrated skin.
- **Suitable for:** all skin types as a night cream.

## How to Use

Apply morning and/or night as the last step before sunscreen (morning).

## Approved Claims

- "Clinically tested: +68% skin hydration after 24 hours." (Study LB-2025-07, n = 32 — substantiates CMP-002.)
- "Helps support the skin's moisture barrier."
- "Soothes the feeling of dry, tight skin."

## Claims Not Allowed

- "Heals eczema", "treats rosacea" or any disease claim (CMP-001).
