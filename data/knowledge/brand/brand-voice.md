# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Positioning

Lumi Botanics is clean, science-backed skincare for people who want results without the hype. We sound like a knowledgeable friend, not a lab coat and not an influencer.

## Tone

### Do

- **Warm and reassuring** — "Your skin, just calmer."
- **Clear and specific** — name the ingredient and what it does.
- **Honest** — say "helps" and "the look of"; never overpromise.
- **Inclusive** — all skin tones, ages and genders.

### Don't

- No fear-based or shaming language ("flaws", "ugly", "fix your face") — see BRD-001.
- No miracle language ("overnight transformation", "say goodbye forever").
- No medical claims — we are a cosmetics brand (CMP-001).
- No ALL CAPS hype or more than one exclamation mark per piece.

## Preferred Words

| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| glow, radiance | flawless |
| the look of blemishes | acne, zits |
| supports, helps | fixes, cures |
| skin-loving | anti-aging |
| routine, ritual | regimen |

## Sample Lines

- "Brightness you can see, ingredients you can read."
- "Gentle on skin, serious about results."
- "Clean formulas, backed by science."
