# Visual Identity Guidelines

## Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Lumi Sage | #9CAF88 | Primary, packaging |
| Morning Cream | #F6F1E7 | Backgrounds |
| Citrus Glow | #F2B544 | Accent — Vitamin C line only |
| Deep Moss | #2F3E2C | Text, logo |

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Fraunces (serif), sentence case.
- **Body:** Inter, 16 px minimum on web.
- Never more than two typefaces in one asset.

## Imagery

### Do

- Real, unretouched skin texture; diverse models.
- Natural daylight, soft shadows, botanical props.
- Product shown in use (hands, bathroom shelf).

### Don't

- No heavy skin retouching or filters.
- No before/after images without the "Results may vary." disclaimer (CMP-003).
- No clinical or hospital settings (implies medical claims).

## Logo

- Clear space = height of the "L" on all sides.
- Deep Moss on light backgrounds, Morning Cream on dark.
