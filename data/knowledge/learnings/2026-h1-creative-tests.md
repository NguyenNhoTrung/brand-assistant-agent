# H1 2026 Creative Test Learnings

## Summary

Across 46 A/B tests on TikTok and Meta (Jan–Jun 2026, $310k spend), **UGC problem–solution** videos beat polished studio ads on click-through rate.

## Format Results

| Format | Avg CTR | CPA | vs. studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| UGC problem–solution | 1.9% | $21 | +58% CTR |
| UGC routine / GRWM | 1.5% | $25 | +25% CTR |
| Studio product beauty shot | 1.2% | $31 | baseline |
| Static carousel | 0.8% | $38 | -33% CTR |

## Hook Learnings

### First 3 seconds

- The hook must land in the **first 3 seconds**; ads with the product or problem visible by second 3 held 2x more viewers.
- Best hooks: texture close-up ("watch this sink in"), relatable problem ("my serum pills under makeup").
- On-screen text in the first frame lifted thumb-stop rate by 18%.

### What did not work

- Generic "glow up" hooks.
- Starting with the logo.

## Recommendations

- Default to UGC problem–solution for TikTok and Reels.
- Pair with the Q2 2026 texture insight: show "non-sticky under makeup".
