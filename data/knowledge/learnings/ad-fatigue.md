# Ad Fatigue Playbook

## Signals of Fatigue

- Frequency above **3.0** per user per 7 days (Meta) or 2.5 (TikTok).
- CTR drops more than **25%** from its first-week average.
- CPA rises more than 20% for 3 consecutive days.

## When to Refresh

| Platform | Typical creative lifespan |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 7–10 days |
| Meta (Reels/Feed) | 2–4 weeks |
| YouTube | 4–6 weeks |

## How to Refresh

### Cheap iterations first

1. New hook (first 3 seconds) on the same body.
2. New on-screen text or caption.
3. New creator delivering the same script.

### Full refresh

New concept when two iterations in a row fail to recover CTR.

## Planning Rule

Keep at least 3 live variants per ad set and have the next batch ready one week before projected fatigue.
