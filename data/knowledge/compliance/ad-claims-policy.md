# Advertising Claims Policy

This policy is the human-readable version of `data/compliance/rules.yaml` (version 2026-09-01). It applies to all ads, social posts, product pages, emails and creative briefs. **Block** rules must be fixed before publishing; **warn** rules need a reviewer's sign-off.

## CMP-001 — No medical or drug claims (block)

Our products are cosmetics. Copy must not claim to cure, treat, heal or prevent any disease or condition (acne, eczema, rosacea, skin cancer).

- Not allowed: "cures acne", "heals eczema", "treats rosacea", "eliminates acne".
- Compliant alternative: "helps reduce the look of blemishes".

## CMP-002 — Clinical claims need substantiation (block)

"Clinically proven" or "clinically tested" may only be used when a study is listed under `clinical_studies` in the product sheet, and the claim must match the study result.

- Vitamin C Serum and Gentle Gel Cleanser: **no studies** — clinical claims not allowed.
- Barrier Repair Cream: study LB-2025-07 — "Clinically tested: +68% skin hydration after 24 hours" allowed.

## CMP-003 — Testimonials need a disclaimer (block)

Any testimonial, customer quote or before/after visual must include the phrase: **"Results may vary."**

## CMP-004 — No absolute superlatives or guarantees (warn)

Avoid "best in the world", "#1 on the market", "number one", "guaranteed results". Use specific, provable statements instead ("loved by 12,000 customers").

## CMP-005 — SPF usage statement (block)

All SPF 50 Daily Fluid communication must include, verbatim: **"Reapply every 2 hours and after swimming."**

## CMP-006 — Discount cap (block)

Promotions must not exceed **25% off** without written CMO approval. Bundles count as the effective discount.

## BRD-001 — No fear-based or shaming language (warn)

Do not use "flaws", "ugly", "fix your face/skin" or similar shaming language. See Brand Voice Guidelines.

## Escalation

Unclear cases go to legal-compliance via the #claims-review channel. Response SLA: 2 business days.
